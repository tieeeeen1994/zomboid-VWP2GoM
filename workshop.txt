title=VWP2GoM - Vanilla Weapons Plus to Guns of Marz
description=Moves an existing save from Vanilla Weapons Plus - Gunworks Edition to Guns of Marz without losing anything. Guns, magazines, attachments, bayonets, rounds, boxes and cartons are converted, including in places you have not visited yet. Vanilla Weapons Plus reworks the vanilla firearms, so vanilla guns, magazines, ammo and attachments are converted too.
description=
description=[b]Warning:[/b] items that only exist in Vanilla Weapons Plus (Glock 17, M4, AK-47, 7.62x39mm rounds...) are deleted the first time a save loads with neither Vanilla Weapons Plus nor VWP2GoM Placeholders enabled. Once the game saves, they cannot be recovered. Back up first and follow the steps in order.
description=
description=[h1]What it converts[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Guns[/b] become their Guns of Marz counterpart. Guns with no counterpart become the Guns of Marz gun with the closest stats: Glock 17 to M92FS, .44 Desert Eagle to S&W 629, SR-25 to PSG1, sawn-off pump to Remington 870, JS-556 to M16A3, JS5 to MP5. Vanilla revolvers become a random Guns of Marz revolver that fires the same round.
description=[*][b]The gun keeps its state:[/b] loaded and chambered rounds, jams, spent casings, fire mode (if the new gun has it), condition (scaled to the new maximum), repairs, blood, folded stock, bipod, fixed bayonet, custom name, favourite, and where it was: hands, back, holster, hotbar, bag, container, vehicle, corpse or floor.
description=[*][b]Attachments[/b] are refitted as the Guns of Marz part that does the same job, with any rail or mount it needs.
description=[*][b]Magazines and ammo[/b] become Guns of Marz magazines, rounds, boxes and cartons with the same number of rounds.
description=[/list]
description=Nothing is thrown away. Rounds that no longer fit, a magazine the new gun cannot take, or an attachment with nowhere to go are placed beside the converted gun.
description=
description=[h1]Before you start[/h1]
description=Subscribe to:
description=[list]
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3722064198]Gunworks-gang[/url] (mod ID SWMG)
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3722134990]Guns of Marz[/url] (mod ID GunsOfMarz). Do not enable [i]Guns of Marz (Old Version)[/i], which is in the same item.
description=[*]This item. It contains two mods and you need both: [b]VWP2GoM - Vanilla Weapons Plus to Guns of Marz[/b] (VWP2GoM) and [b]VWP2GoM Placeholders[/b] (VWP2GoM_Placeholders).
description=[/list]
description=Stay subscribed to Vanilla Weapons Plus until you have finished, so you can still load your backup. The Zomboid folder below is in your user folder, e.g. C:\Users\<you>\Zomboid.
description=
description=[h1]Single player[/h1]
description=[olist]
description=[*]Quit the game.
description=[*]Back up the save: copy Zomboid/Saves/<game mode>/<save name> (e.g. Zomboid/Saves/Apocalypse/MySave) to somewhere outside the Zomboid folder.
description=[*]Turn debug mode off: in Steam, right-click Project Zomboid > Properties and remove -debug from Launch Options. In debug mode VWP2GoM closes the game before the world loads, so nothing is lost, but nothing is converted.
description=[*]Start the game. Click [b]LOAD[/b], select the save, click [b]MORE...[/b], then [b]Choose Mods...[/b].
description=[*]Disable [b]Vanilla Weapons Plus - Gunworks Edition[/b].
description=[*]Enable [b]Gunworks-gang[/b], [b]Guns of Marz[/b], [b]VWP2GoM - Vanilla Weapons Plus to Guns of Marz[/b] and [b]VWP2GoM Placeholders[/b].
description=[*]Click [b]ACCEPT[/b], then [b]PLAY[/b]. Items are converted while the save loads.
description=[*]Check the log (see below) before you load the save again. Each load starts a new log.
description=[*]Keep all four mods enabled for this save from now on.
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h1]Dedicated server[/h1]
description=[olist]
description=[*]Stop the server.
description=[*]Back up Zomboid/Saves/Multiplayer/<server name> to somewhere outside the Zomboid folder. It includes players.db, which holds every player's inventory.
description=[*]Open Zomboid/Server/<server name>.ini in a text editor.
description=[*]On the [b]Mods=[/b] line, remove MarzVanillaGuns and add SWMG, GunsOfMarz, VWP2GoM and VWP2GoM_Placeholders. Keep your other mods, separated by ;
description=[code]Mods=SWMG;GunsOfMarz;VWP2GoM;VWP2GoM_Placeholders[/code]
description=[*]On the [b]WorkshopItems=[/b] line, remove 3773834525 and add 3722064198, 3722134990 and 3803058865.
description=[code]WorkshopItems=3722064198;3722134990;3803058865[/code]
description=[*]Start the server without -debug. In debug mode VWP2GoM shuts the server down before the world loads.
description=[*]Check Zomboid/Lua/VWP2GoM.log on the server machine before restarting it.
description=[*]Keep all four mods in the server's mod list from now on.
description=[/olist]
description=The conversion runs on the server. Players download the mods when they join and need to do nothing else; each inventory is converted when its player joins. If you host from the main menu with HOST, follow the same steps; the settings file is Zomboid/Server/servertest.ini unless you renamed the server.
description=
description=[h1]Checking the log[/h1]
description=Zomboid/Lua/VWP2GoM.log is cleared every time a world loads. Its first line names the world, then there is one line per converted item:
description=[code]Base.PistolGlock [cond 7/10, ammo 12+1, mag] -> MarzGuns.M92FS [cond 7/10, ammo 12+1, mag] | none on Player at 10862,9412,0[/code]
description=Items placed beside the new one are listed after a +. Search the log for:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]FAILED[/b]: that item was left unchanged. It is retried the next time its area loads, or every in-game minute if it is in a player's inventory. If it keeps failing, post the line and Zomboid/console.txt in the comments.
description=[*][b]STOPPED[/b]: debug mode was on. Restart without -debug.
description=[*][b]Guns of Marz or Gunworks is not loaded[/b]: nothing was converted. Enable Gunworks-gang and Guns of Marz (not the Old Version).
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Why the mods must stay enabled[/h1]
description=VWP2GoM converts items when the game loads them: player inventories when the save loads or the player joins (and every in-game minute after), containers, floors and corpses when their area loads, vehicles when they load, and new loot when it is generated.
description=
description=Areas nobody has visited since the switch still hold the old items, and VWP2GoM Placeholders keeps them from being deleted until they are converted. If you disable either VWP2GoM mod, those items are lost the next time their area loads.
description=
description=[h1]If something went wrong[/h1]
description=If the save was loaded without VWP2GoM Placeholders, or you want to go back to Vanilla Weapons Plus: quit, delete the save folder, copy your backup back in its place and start again. VWP2GoM cannot convert a save back.
description=
description=[h1]FAQ[/h1]
description=[b]Why are new guns I find random?[/b] Guns of Marz's sandbox options [i]Enable Vanilla Weapon/Ammo/Attachments Replacement[/i] replace vanilla items in new loot with random Guns of Marz ones. VWP2GoM leaves that loot to Guns of Marz and converts everything else with its fixed table.
description=
description=[b]Why did vanilla rounds I crafted turn into Gunworks rounds?[/b] While VWP2GoM is enabled, it converts vanilla guns, magazines, ammo and attachments in your inventory every in-game minute.
description=
