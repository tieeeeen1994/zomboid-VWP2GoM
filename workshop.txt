title=VWP2GoM - Vanilla Weapons Plus to Guns of Marz
description=Moves an existing save from Vanilla Weapons Plus - Gunworks Edition to Guns of Marz without losing anything. Every gun, magazine, attachment, round, box and carton is converted, including in places you have not visited yet.
description=
description=[h1]What it converts[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Guns[/b] become their Guns of Marz counterpart. Guns with no counterpart become the Guns of Marz gun with the closest stats: Glock 17 to M92FS, .44 Desert Eagle to S&W 629, SR-25 to PSG1, sawn-off pump to Remington 870, JS-556 to M16A3, JS5 to MP5.
description=[*][b]The gun keeps its state:[/b] loaded rounds and chambered round, jams, spent shells, fire mode, condition, repairs, custom name, favourite, and where it was: hands, back, holster, container or floor.
description=[*][b]Attachments[/b] are refitted as the Guns of Marz part that does the same job, with any rail or muzzle mount it needs.
description=[*][b]Magazines and ammo[/b] become Guns of Marz magazines, rounds, boxes and cartons with exactly the same number of rounds.
description=[/list]
description=Nothing is thrown away. Rounds that no longer fit, a magazine a revolver cannot take, or an attachment with nowhere to go are placed beside the converted gun.
description=
description=[h1]How to migrate[/h1]
description=[olist]
description=[*]Back up your save.
description=[*]Disable Vanilla Weapons Plus. Enable Guns of Marz, VWP2GoM and VWP2GoM Placeholders. Load the save.
description=[*]Check Zomboid/Lua/VWP2GoM.log. Every conversion is one line.
description=[*]Keep both VWP2GoM mods enabled. Areas you have not visited yet are converted when they first load.
description=[/olist]
description=VWP2GoM Placeholders keeps Vanilla Weapons Plus items from being deleted when the save loads without that mod. Enable it in the same load you disable Vanilla Weapons Plus, never together with it.
description=
description=[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
description=Build 42. Requires Gunworks-gang and Guns of Marz. Multiplayer: install on the server and every client; the conversion runs on the server.
